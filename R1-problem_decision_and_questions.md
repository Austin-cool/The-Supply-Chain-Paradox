### R1. Problem, questions, and goals

In this work, we aim to identify the root cause of a phenomenon observed at local grocery stores. In essence, the data gathered by the stores shows that, during the store's biggest sales, profit margins collapse and shelves go empty for items that are not on sale. This observation, termed the "empty shelves paradox," has led managers to conclude that sales need to be abolished. However, there may be a different reason for this phenomenon. Thus, the main problem we seek to tackle is

**Identify the cause of low profits and empty shelves during big sale weeks.**

To address the above problem, we first analyzed the input from the experts who run the chain. Through their inputs, we extracted valuable information that will guide our study. First, we found that, although sales run weekly, there are instances (e.g., during holidays) where the store holds a larger sale, which shows us that the profit may be dependent on the calendar day. Additionally, we found that deliveries to restock each department run on fixed schedules. However, each department receives deliveries with different frequencies. Moreover, during an ad week, the items on sale are prioritized. This observation shows that truck deliveries and their schedules have an influence on product availability and, thus, product margins. From these observations, our team would like to explore how exact the products on sale are, affecting product margins. Similarly, we look to understand how delivery schedules influence product shortages during big sale days.

In particular, our team will answer the following questions, where questions 1-3 are concerned with profit margins, and questions 4 and 5 are concerned with product availability.

**Question 1:** How much does the truck capacity affect product shortages?

**Goal:** Understand if truck capacity affects product margins.

**Question 2:** During a big-sale event, which department has the lowest margins?

**Goal:** Understand the profit per department during sales.

**Question 3:** How much profit is the store getting for products on sale?

**Goal:** Understand where the profit collapse is coming from.

**Question 4:** How does product availability change for products on sale and products not on sale?

**Goal:** Understand availability in reference to sales

**Question 5:** When are profit margins collapsing?

**Goal:** Understand if profit collapse happens for all ad weeks or only for big sales, such as holidays.
